## Featured Project

### ForecastGuard – Project Cost Risk Assistant

ForecastGuard is an AI assistant developed during the AI Lab activity in Machine Learning Fundamentals. The assistant is designed to support Project Controls Analysts by identifying potential forecast risks before they become cost overruns.

The project included research with different AI tools, assistant design, prompt development, scenario testing, tuning, and validation. ForecastGuard reviews information such as actual costs, accruals, commitments, Estimate to Complete, Estimate at Completion, physical progress, productivity, and schedule exposure.

During testing, I identified that the initial version sometimes confused missing information with actual project risk. I modified the instructions to separate Project Cost Health from Forecast Confidence and then tested the assistant again. The revised version produced a more appropriate assessment.

This project helped me understand that AI systems should not simply be accepted because their responses sound reasonable. Domain knowledge, testing, and iteration are necessary to determine whether an AI solution is producing useful and reliable results.
